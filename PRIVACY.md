# Privacy Policy

**InstaTube** (`com.roshan.instatube`), developed by **Roshan**.

## What we collect

Nothing. InstaTube has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no telemetry, and no third-party tracking servers.

## How data is handled on your device

- Instagram and YouTube links you paste or share are resolved directly from your device to fetch media streams.
- Downloaded videos, audio tracks, and cover images are saved locally to your device (`Downloads/InstaTube` by default, or your chosen folder).
- Optional YouTube sign-in credentials and app preferences are stored locally on your device.

## Permissions

- **Internet & Network State**: Fetch media streams and detect Data Saver mode.
- **Storage (Android 9 and below)**: Save downloaded files to the public `Downloads` folder.
- **Foreground Service & Notifications**: Complete background downloads and support background media playback.
- **Vibrate**: Provide tactile haptic feedback.
