# Privacy Policy

**InstaTube** (`dev.xboyrex.instatube`), developed by **ㅤᏞᴀᴢʏㅤᎷꫝʏᴀɴк まやんく (`Mayank · @xboyrex`)**.

- **Zero Telemetry & Zero Analytics**: InstaTube does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, analytics, or crash logs to any external servers.
- **Direct Media Extraction**: Network requests are made directly from your device to Instagram and YouTube endpoints solely to resolve and download the media links you request.
- **Local Storage Only**: All downloaded videos, audio tracks, and covers are stored locally on your device in your selected Vault directory (`Downloads/InstaTube`).
